Conference · Tainan, Taiwan

IUMRS-ICA 2026

Students of the Computational Materials Unit presented first-principles research on hydrogen storage, battery anodes, and gas sensing at IUMRS-ICA 2026 at ICC Tainan.

Date
31 Oct–4 November 2026
Location
Tainan, Taiwan

Event gallery

Science and community in Tainan

Audomsak Sripothongnack, Watcharin Teeranattapong, and Tawan Paensuk shared work on doped BNC₂ for hydrogen storage, δ-phographene as a lithium- and sodium-ion battery anode, and Janus HfSSe and ZrSSe monolayers for gas sensing, through an oral talk and the poster sessions.
