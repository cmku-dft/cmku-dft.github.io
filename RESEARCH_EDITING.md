# Research section editorial policy

Confirmed by Pakpoom Reunchan, 11 September 2026.

- Prioritize papers where Pakpoom is first author, explicitly marked corresponding author, or last author when choosing core research themes, highlighted papers, and figures.
- Last-author status qualifies independently; do not equate it with corresponding authorship. Check asterisks/symbols against the paper's correspondence footnote.
- Middle-author collaborations without confirmed correspondence remain in the full publication archive and may support a smaller collaborations section. They should not by themselves establish a main research theme.
- Current main themes: defects and electronic structure; hydrogen storage; two-dimensional gas sensing; photocatalytic materials and band engineering; beyond-lithium batteries (ongoing).
- Beyond-lithium batteries has no published group result yet, according to Pakpoom. Do not present collaborative Li-diffusion or proton-conductivity papers as beyond-lithium publications.
- Preserve the complete bibliography. The homepage Recent Work remains exactly the five latest publications, independent of this research-theme selection rule.
